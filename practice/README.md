# Practice Questions

All questions are original and scenario-style, modelled on the published domains. They are **not** real exam items. Each domain set has 15 questions (allow 30 minutes) and ends with a quick answer key.

**246 questions in total:** a 21-question diagnostic plus 3 sets per domain.

Also: the [Diagnostic](questions.md), 21 mixed scenario questions across all domains (40 minutes).

## How to use them
1. Take the **Diagnostic** first, without notes.
2. For each domain, in the order of the [study plan](../study-plan.md):
   - Do **Set 1** after reading the domain notes.
   - Do **Set 2** after the relevant lab.
   - Keep **Set 3** for the end of the week as a check.
3. Mark your answers with the quick answer key, then read the explanation for every question you got wrong *and* every one you guessed.
4. Record your scores in [../progress.md](../progress.md). Re-take any set where you scored under 80% a week later.
5. **Mock exam:** draw 60 questions across the sets in the exam weighting (D1 16, D3 12, D4 12, D2 11, D5 9) and allow 120 minutes.
